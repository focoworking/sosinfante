Privacy policy

How SOS Infante collects, uses and protects your personal information on www.sosinfante.org and its subdomains.

What we collect

Information you give us voluntarily through donation, volunteer, contact and newsletter forms: name, email, phone, city and professional background. Technical data collected automatically: IP address, device type, browser, pages visited and traffic source.

How we use it

To process donations and issue receipts; to manage volunteer applications; to answer your questions; to send the newsletter when you opt in; and to measure how the site performs, in aggregate.

Cookies

We use first-party cookies for basic preferences and analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can block them in your browser without losing access to any content.

Who we share it with

Only the providers we need to operate: payment processor, transactional email, analytics and hosting. We do not sell or trade personal data.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without the consent of a parent or guardian. Images of children are published only with informed consent, and with their identity protected by default.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data by writing to accion@sosinfante.org. We respond within 30 days.

Questions about these policies: accion@sosinfante.org.
