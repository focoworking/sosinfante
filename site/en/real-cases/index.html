Real cases

Real cases

This section does not tell stories to move you: it documents mechanisms. What made it possible, what failed afterward, and what would have to change so it does not happen again. Every figure carries its source and its date.

This section deals with violence against children. If a child is at risk right now, call emergency services before reading on: 911 in the United States and Venezuela, ECU 911 in Ecuador. In the US you can also reach the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline at 1-800-422-4453.

The numbers

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

Every 90 minutes

Ecuador's Attorney General logged 86,962 reports of sexual offenses against minors between 2015 and 2025. The number that matters isn't the total. It's the pace.

Venezuela · 2026-10-21

Counted by hand

With no current official statistics, the 2025 count in Zulia state had to be assembled by tracking social media posts. Even so, the pattern of who harms children came through clearly.

Venezuela · 2026-10-21

The data stops here

The last national figure released covers January 2022 to May 2023. What came after was never published, and that absence is itself a finding.

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

Four births a day

Ecuador's statistics agency published the figure in August 2026. Nearly four daily births to girls who, by age, cannot legally consent to sex.

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

The map says it

Morona Santiago nearly triples the national average. Ecuador's map of child pregnancy matches its map of distance to a hospital.

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

The wrong label

"88 girls give birth every day" circulated as a headline. The number is real, but it does not say what it appears to. That gap is a gift to anyone denying the problem.

Venezuela · 2026-10-21

Twenty thousand posts

A journalistic investigation found thousands of posts tied to the grooming of minors on a single social network. The state publishes no data on this.

Latin America · 2026-10-21

81 against 19

The 2025 regional report quantifies a gap that should shape any prevention campaign: online risk is not distributed evenly.

Violence at home

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

No stranger at the door

Ecuador's Attorney General estimates that 85 to 90 percent of offenders come from the family or the circle of known adults. That changes where prevention belongs.

Practical guide · 2026-10-21

Age by age

What counts as a warning sign in a four-year-old may be perfectly normal in a twelve-year-old. This guide separates them by stage.

Practical guide · 2026-10-21

They tell you once

What the first adult who listens does determines much of what follows, both in the legal process and in the child's recovery.

Schools and authority

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

Six thousand files

Human Rights Watch documented a decade of sexual violence inside Ecuador's school system. The problem wasn't only what happened. It was what schools did next.

Ecuador and Venezuela · 2026-10-21

Who gets called first

There is a practical difference between a school protocol that works and one that merely exists. The tell is who gets notified first.

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

Don't take the phone

Grooming reports in Ecuador grew fivefold in a decade. What matters is not the growth but what it reveals about where first contact now happens.

Ecuador and Venezuela · 2026-10-21

Three sentences

62.4 percent of surveyed Ecuadorian adolescents recognize sextortion as a risk. Almost none know the correct response is the opposite of their first instinct.

Faith institutions

Ecuador and Venezuela · 2026-10-21

The same four steps

Documented cases across the region share a recognizable sequence. Understanding it is more useful than being outraged at each episode separately.

Ecuador, Venezuela and the United States · 2026-10-21

Four questions

They work the same for a school, a sports club, a youth group or a parish. They take five minutes to answer and reveal almost everything.

Justice and impunity

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

4.15 percent

Of 52,051 cases reported over five and a half years, 2,161 reached a verdict. The rest describes what actually happens to a family that decides to come forward.

Ecuador · 2026-10-21

A target already met

The national policy aims to reach a rate of 1.81 within ten years. The 2025 registry already recorded 1.8. That arithmetic deserves a closer look.

Ecuador and Venezuela · 2026-10-21

Words that hide

Some everyday phrases in the press and in conversation turn a crime into an event with no one responsible. Changing them is not political correctness. It is precision.
